# Pivot Point Labs

At Pivot Point Labs, we create Assetto Corsa tracks and apps, both on commission and independently. We care about getting the details right, from the smallest assets to the way everything works together. Our focus is on creating quality content that feels well-made, works as intended, and reflects the time and effort we put into each project.

## The Team 

Pivot Point Labs consists of two members, Killball3000 (KB3K) and SLX, combining 3D asset creation, track development, and technical implementation to bring our Assetto Corsa projects to life.

Killball3000 focuses on 3D asset creation using Blender and Blockbench, alongside texture creation, foliage, and various track-related configurations and settings.

SLX also works on 3D asset creation in Blender, with a stronger focus on the technical side of development. This includes in-depth configuration work, custom generators, templates and scripts, as well as Lua programming.

## The Purpose

Track development involves a lot more than just making things look good. Getting a track to run well takes just as much care, from keeping mesh and draw counts under control to setting up proper culling and optimizing assets.

This wiki is meant to bring all that knowledge together in one place. It covers the techniques, workflows, scripts and tools we use to build tracks that look good without sacrificing performance. The goal is to make useful information easier to find, share what we've learned along the way, and save others from having to figure everything out from scratch.
